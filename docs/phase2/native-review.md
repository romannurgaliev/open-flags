# Phase 2 — Questions for native speakers and cultural experts

Simplifying an inscription, coat of arms or ornament can be offensive or simply wrong. People from the country decide this, not the designer. Until they answer, these miniatures stay `draft` or `blocked-review` and are left out of releases.

Each question comes with the full flag, the S/M/L miniatures at actual size and at ×4, and the simplification rule from [05](../05-simplification-rules.md).

## SA — Saudi Arabia (blocks the release)

1. At 16–40 px the shahada is replaced by a schematic of white lines of different lengths. Is this acceptable, or does the schematic read as a distortion of sacred text?
2. If the schematic is not acceptable, what is acceptable at 16 px: (a) the sword only, (b) the real calligraphy scaled down without simplification, even if illegible, or (c) no SA miniatures at all, leaving a gap in the set?
3. Is the sword's direction (hilt on the right, blade toward the hoist) correct in the miniatures?

The same questions will apply to flags outside the pilot: IQ (takbir), IR (takbir along the stripe edges), AF.

## KZ — Kazakhstan

1. At 16 px the hoist ornament is a 1 px toothed column. Is it recognizable as the ornament, or does it look like a mistake?
2. Sun: 8 rays at 16 px, 16 at 24, 32 at 40. Does reducing the rays change the symbol's meaning?
3. Which yellow: lemon (Pantone 102U per ST RK 988-2007, as quoted) or golden, as on most real flags? Is the text of the standard available?

## TM — Turkmenistan

1. The five carpet guls on the hoist band are replaced by dots (16 px) and diamonds (24–40 px). Is this acceptable? The guls stand for tribes, and their order matters.
2. Should the order of the guls be kept at least at 40 px?

## BR — Brazil

1. The motto "ORDEM E PROGRESSO" is dropped in every miniature; the band stays. Is this accepted (almost every set does it), or does it read as an incomplete flag?
2. The stars at 24 and 40 px are a few dots, not the legal positions. Is there a constellation that must stay readable (the Southern Cross)?

## MX — Mexico

1. At 16 px the eagle is a brown blot with a green line. Is it recognizable as the coat of arms, or would another silhouette work better?

## ES — Spain

1. At 16 px the coat of arms is dropped: the Constitution allows the civil flag without it. Does the flag without the arms read as the "real" one?

## VA — Vatican City

1. At 16 px the keys are two diagonals (gold and silver) and the tiara is a blot. Is that enough?

## Where to find people

Type designers working with Arabic script (for SA, IQ, IR), local design communities in Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan, and vexillology communities (FOTW, the Flag Institute) for questions about symbolism. Each answer is recorded in the flag's metadata (`review`) with a name and date.
