A pilot, not a release.

- 25 flags: AU, BD, BR, CH, CY, ES, FJ, ID, JP, KZ, MC, MX, MY, NP, NZ, PL, PW, QA, RO, SA, TD, TM, US, VA, XK.
- 12 formats: original, 3x2, 1x1; square, circle and rect at tiers 16, 24, 40.
- Specification in `docs/`: sources and color rules, inclusion and semver, license, metadata, formats and tiers, simplification rules, confusable pairs, checks, contributing, phase 2 plan.
- Checks C01–C16 and 22 negative tests; GitHub Actions for the checks and for the Figma export.

- Full formats still in draft: BR, CY, ES, FJ, KZ, MX, SA, TM, VA, XK. SA miniatures are waiting for review by native speakers.
- All public text is in English.
